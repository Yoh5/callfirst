Be Margaret's front door for a few minutes.

You get your own household: Margaret, a doorbell, a chime, a sensor on the front door, and Sarah, who is told. There is no real doorbell, so the door is played from buttons. Everything behind them is real: each press goes to a Ring Partner API emulator, which sends a signed webhook to Call First, which decides exactly as it would for a real home.

  1. Someone rings. Nobody was expected: your speakers play the chime's reminder.
  2. Open the door. Sarah's page shows the alert, with the doorbell's still image and a report sheet.
  3. Pause the protection, or paste a fake delivery message asking to "collect gold for safekeeping". See what Sarah is told, and what is refused.

Your demo household is deleted after two hours. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; emails are written to the server, not delivered.
