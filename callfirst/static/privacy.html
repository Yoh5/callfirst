Call First

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 September 2026. Plain words, because the people it protects should be able to read it.

Who decides

The resident — the Ring customer — switches Call First on, chooses up to three trusted people, and can pause it, switch it off, remove anyone, or delete everything. Nobody else can switch it on for them. Every change is told to the trusted people.

What we collect, and why

  • Doorbell events from Ring: someone rang, a person was seen, the door sensor opened, the doorbell went offline. Times only. Used to decide whether a visitor was expected and whether the door was answered.
  • One still image from the doorbell, fetched only when an alert is sent, and attached to that alert. No video is downloaded. No live view is opened.
  • What the resident declares: expected visits (a label, days and times), expected deliveries (carrier, tracking number, window), and the trusted people's names and email addresses.
  • A pasted delivery message is read to fill in the delivery form. If a language model is used to read it, the message text is sent to that model provider for that purpose only, and is not kept by Call First after the delivery is declared.

What we never do

  • Recognise faces, compare images, or try to identify any visitor.
  • Contact the police or any agency. The report sheet is prepared for the family, who decide whether to file it.
  • Sell, share or use any of it for advertising.
  • Put the resident's schedule in an alert. Trusted people see alerts, never the times the resident is usually alone.

Who sees what

  • The resident: everything.
  • Trusted people: whether protection is on, the alerts and their images, the report sheets. Each has a personal link; the resident can revoke it.
  • Ring: the requests Call First makes to play the chime and fetch an image.
  • An email provider: the alert emails it carries.

How it is kept

Everything about a household — people, times, alerts, images, the event journal — is encrypted at rest. Access links are stored only as hashes. Webhooks from Ring are accepted only with Ring's signature.

How long

Until the resident deletes it. Delete everything on the resident's page removes all of it at once; the trusted people are told.

Contact

Questions or requests: the address shown in the Ring Appstore listing.
